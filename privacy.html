Privacy Policy

ClipsGuru · Last updated November 19, 2026

ClipsGuru is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @clipsguru855. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other person.

What data the app touches

  • Video files: gameplay recordings supplied directly by the owner, processed entirely on the owner's own computer.
  • TikTok access token: an OAuth token issued by TikTok after the owner signs in and approves access, used only to upload the owner's own clips via TikTok's official API. It is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's own API.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any other individual.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use TikTok data for advertising or analytics purposes.

Data retention

Video files and the access token are kept locally for as long as the owner chooses to keep them, and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local files.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the tool changes.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account holder of @clipsguru855.
